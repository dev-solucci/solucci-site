How to use

Quick reference on top, reasoning underneath. Nobody has to read it end to end.

Chapters 01 to 03 answer who Solucci is. Use them to write a proposal, introduce the house to a new client or decide whether a project fits. Chapters 04 to 06 answer how the brand looks: logo, colour and type, with the measurements and the files. Chapters 07 and 08 answer how the brand talks, from a WhatsApp message to a website statement. Chapter 09 shows the brand assembled in real pieces, and 10 hands over the files.

None of this is set in stone, and none of it is decoration either. When a rule gets in the way of real work, change the rule in this document instead of quietly making an exception. Brands do not lose consistency in one big decision, they lose it in thirty small ones.

The short version, if you have thirty seconds

  • One sentenceSolucci turns a stuck problem into a solution that stands on its own, built with a designer's care and a developer's backbone.
  • One action colourCoral #F5533D. Teal for tools and states, ink for everything structural.
  • One display faceArchivo, italic and wide, uppercase. It is what rhymes with the tilt of the logo.
  • One way of talkingPerson to person. "We will sort it out", never "our company delivers solutions".
01

Platform

The why before the how. Everything that follows rests on this.

The idea in one sentence

Solucci turns a stuck problem into a solution that stands on its own, built with a designer's care and a developer's backbone.

Solucci transforma o problema travado em solução que se sustenta sozinha, com o cuidado de quem desenha e a firmeza de quem constrói.

Every word is doing a job. Stands on its own says the work runs without crutches, with a light echo of a horse holding itself upright. A designer's care is design, a developer's backbone is dev, the two fronts behind almost everything the house makes.

Purpose, vision and mission

  • PurposeTaking good ideas off the page and carrying them forward, with design, technology and soul. Plenty of people know what they want and have nobody to build it with craft and care. Solucci is that bridge.Tirar boas ideias do papel e levá-las adiante.
  • VisionTo be known as a Brazilian creative house that solves across languages, from design to code, from games to music, proving an independent studio can carry a company's solidity and a collective's soul. Start in Brazil, speak to the world.
  • MissionSolve brand, product and digital experience problems at a level of craft most people save for big accounts, and deliver it with clarity, closeness and on time.

Values

Five principles hold the house up. They are not wall decoration, they are the tiebreaker when a project gets tight.

  • Craft over speedWork ships beautiful and well made because that is how we would want to receive it. Finish is part of the job, not an add-on.
  • Solve, do not just deliverThe job ends when the client's problem is gone, not when the file is exported.
  • Authorial, unafraid of a facePersonality is the edge, not the trim. A brand without a face ends up competing on price alone.
  • People before projectClients are people and so is the team. Level conversation, open process, no corporate fog.
  • One house, many doorsEach division speaks its own language, and all of them carry the same DNA and the same word up front.

Essence

Solutions with soul.

Solução com alma.

Two halves in one line. Solutions is the firm ground, the problem actually gone. Soul is what most people leave out: the care, the personality, the extra thing that makes a client remember.

02

Architecture

One strong parent brand and four divisions that borrow its strength and pay it back.

The model

Solucci runs as a branded house. Not an invisible holding company sheltering loose brands, the opposite: the Solucci name is the main asset and every division is endorsed by it.

It compounds, because every job in every area builds the same name. Six years of reputation do not split four ways, they stack into one. It signals structure without forcing you to invent four companies. It is cheap to scale, since opening a division means adding one word to the system. And it cross-sells: whoever comes in through design finds out there is dev too.

The divisions

Solucci Design

Brand, product and experience.

Visual identity and branding, UX and UI, product design and art direction. The house's visual language, and often the client's first door.

Solucci Dev

Your idea, live.

Sites, web apps, WordPress, front-end, integrations, automations and systems. Where design becomes a product that works.

Solucci Games

Fun, well built.

Digital and analogue, board and card games included. The most playful front in the house.

Solucci Music

A stage for the unheard.

Focused on rap and on a platform for underground artists: production, distribution and a stage. The front closest to culture and community.

Signing off, in practice

Like this

  • Corporate work and cross-area projects sign as Solucci alone.
  • Division work signs Solucci Design, division name after the logo and in a lighter weight.
  • Credit in the wild: por Solucci or by Solucci.
  • One parent domain with per-division paths, like solucci.com.br/design.

Never like this

  • A division name bigger or heavier than Solucci.
  • A separate logo per division. A division is a word, not a new brand.
  • Translating the name. Solucci Design is Solucci Design anywhere.
  • A separate domain per division, which scatters the authority you built.

When a new division is born

Three conditions have to hold at once: there is real and recurring demand, it is a distinct language rather than one more service inside an existing division, and someone is there or will be there to lead it. Names follow Solucci plus one short noun, a single word, pronounceable in both languages, and never louder than Solucci itself.

Possible paths: Solucci Studio for audio, video and motion. Solucci Labs for R&D and experiments. Solucci Print for physical and editorial. Solucci Academy for courses and mentoring.

03

Positioning

For whom, against what, and why pick Solucci over the rest.

Statement

For people with an idea or a problem who do not want to split it across four different vendors, Solucci is the creative technology house that solves across languages, design, development, games and music, with the craft of an independent studio and the trust of six years doing this. Unlike a generic agency or a lone freelancer, Solucci brings personality and solidity in the same package.

Audience

  • PrimarySmall and mid-sized companies and founders, in Portuguese and English, who need a site, a brand, a digital product or a system and want a single partner with a face and some care. They value design, but it has to work.
  • SecondaryOther creatives and studios subcontracting design or dev. Solucci is dependable as white-label and strong as its own brand.
  • CulturalUnderground artists and the games scene, arriving through Music and Games. Not the biggest revenue, but what builds community and affection for the brand.

Value proposition

  • One partner, many languagesNo need to stitch together a Frankenstein of vendors.
  • Studio craft, company deliveryBeautiful and well made, on time, with a clear process.
  • Personality that sticksThe brand has a face of its own, and that is what makes clients remember and refer.
  • Real people on the other sideNo corporate fog and no vanishing halfway through.
04

Logo

One tilted wordmark and one symbol that works alone. Nothing else.

Primary lockup

The official logo is the full wordmark: symbol plus SOLUCCI in a heavy, tilted grotesque. It signs the website, proposals, decks and any piece where someone meets the brand for the first time.

Background

The file ships in currentColor, so a single SVG covers black, white, coral and whatever colour it inherits from its parent. On dark backgrounds the logo is bone #F3F1EC rather than pure white, which buzzes against the ink.

Symbol alone

The symbol is a star-shaped wing cut by a bolt. It goes out on its own when the space is square or too small for the wordmark: favicon, avatar, app icon, badge, footer stamp. Anywhere else, use the wordmark.

Background

Clear space

Call X the height of the logo, which is also the height of the symbol. It needs 1X of clear space on every side, free of text, imagery, borders or other marks. In a tight layout, shrink the logo before eating into the clear space.

1X 1X

Minimum size

  • Wordmark on screen100 px wide. Below that the two C shapes lose their counters and the symbol turns to mush.
  • Wordmark in print26 mm wide. For screen printing or embroidery, go up to 34 mm.
  • Symbol on screen24 px. The 16 px favicon is the one authorised exception, and it uses the dedicated favicon file rather than the raw symbol.
  • Symbol in print8 mm.

Never do this

Each frame below is a real mistake, drawn with the actual file.

Do not stretchAlways scale proportionally.

Do not rotateThe tilt is already drawn in.

No shadowNo outline, bevel or gradient.

Off paletteInk, bone, coral or black. That is it.

No contrastIf it disappears, switch versions.

Do not cropThe logo goes in whole or not at all.

Note on the previous logo

The pixel wordmark, the one with a teal square dotting the i, is retired. It is not an alternate version and not an authorised legacy use. Wherever it still shows up, replace it.

Roles, not preferences

Every colour has a job. When two colours fight over the same job on a screen, one of them is wrong.

  • Ink is structureText, dark backgrounds, the logo. The colour that appears the most and asks for the least attention.
  • Coral is actionPrimary button, highlighted link, the only accent that says "click here". One primary action per screen.
  • Teal is toolingSelection, focus, state indicators, interface handles. Never competes with coral.
  • Bronze, gold and magenta are accentsThey categorise, mark a division, warm up a card. They never become button colours.

Contrast we actually measured

What this forces

  • A coral button carries white text only from 16 px bold up. Below that, use ink text.
  • A coral link inside body text uses deep coral #D63A26 with an underline.
  • Teal, bronze and faint ink are graphic: rules, dots, icons, borders. Not text.

Common mistakes

  • Light grey captions on cream, gone the moment a phone hits daylight.
  • Coral and teal at the same weight on one screen, fighting for attention.
  • A coral to teal gradient. Both are role colours, not decoration.

07

Voice

Personality in layers, not four adjectives stacked at the same height.

The layers

At the same level, the traits turn to soup. Stacked in order, they add up to a coherent person.

  • Base . bold creative soulWhat lands first. The nerve to have a face of its own is what makes the brand memorable.
  • Engine . technical excellenceNone of it holds without real competence underneath. Not just good looking, well built.
  • Spine . professional trustSix years, deadlines, process, promises kept. What lets a big client sleep at night.
  • Voice . close and humanPerson to person, plain and direct.

Tone

Direct, short sentences, one clear idea at a time, respecting the reader's time. Warm instead of corporate. Confident without arrogance, proving command through the work rather than through adjectives, more "look what we made" than "we are the best". Plus a playful edge that never runs over clarity or credibility when the context is serious.

The voice in practice

Solucci sounds like this

  • Shall we sort this out?
  • Your stuck site can breathe again.
  • Design that looks good and works.
  • We handle it from the first idea to live.

Solucci never sounds like this

  • Contact us for a commercial proposal.
  • We optimise your website's performance.
  • High impact synergistic solutions.
  • We offer end-to-end services.

Running in two languages

The brand is born speaking Portuguese and English. Names and divisions are never translated. The tagline has an official pair in both languages, decided in advance and never improvised on the spot. The tone is the same on both sides, and in English it pays to dodge stiff formality to keep the warmth. Context picks the language, and the two never mix in one piece unless that is the point.

Verbal system

Tagline, sign-offs and the pitch you can say in a lift without stumbling.

Primary tagline

Where solutions spring.

A nascente da solução.

It reaches straight for Hippocrene, the spring Pegasus opened with a hoof and where the Muses drew inspiration. It says Solucci is not only the one who delivers the solution, it is where the solution comes from. It works for the parent brand and for any division, and the double meaning survives in English: spring as a source and as the act of springing up.

Rules for every piece

Always

  • One primary action per screen, in coral.
  • Labels in mono, uppercase, tracked out. The house's technical signature.
  • Headlines in Archivo, copy in Plex Sans. Never the other way around.
  • Spacing in multiples of 4 px.

Never

  • Emoji in brand pieces.
  • Generic stock icons crowning every block.
  • Heavy shadows and very round corners, which age fast.
  • Animation that communicates neither state nor direction.

Before you use it

Always start from the SVG. PNG only when a tool refuses vectors. Never redraw the logo, never rebuild it from a screenshot, and never fake the wordmark by typing SOLUCCI in Archivo: the outlines carry their own corrections that no font reproduces.
